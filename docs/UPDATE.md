# Recent Updates

### 1. Expanded World Map Dataset & Country Coverage
- Upgraded world map dataset to Natural Earth 50m resolution, expanding coverage to **240+ countries and territories** (covering all 195 UN member states + observers).
- Added missing island nations and microstates, including **Republic of Maldives**, **Kingdom of Bahrain**, Singapore, Malta, Monaco, Vatican City, Mauritius, Seychelles, Cabo Verde, and more.
- Enhanced location search in sidebar to match official formal names (e.g. *"Republic of Maldives"*, *"Kingdom of Bahrain"*, *"United States of America"*) and common aliases.

### 2. Sign-In Fixes
